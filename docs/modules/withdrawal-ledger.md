# Withdrawal periods and treatment history - requirements ledger

Owner-approved 2026-11-24. The platform is NEUTRAL: it owns and promotes no drug and ships no medicine list; every
figure (treatment days, milk days, meat days) is typed by the vet from the product label and stored with who and when;
the platform only does date arithmetic. Rules (harness H16, `frontend/src/guards/requirementsLedger.guard.test.ts`): a
row never disappears - it changes status; `Built` only with checked evidence; `Deferred` names the owner and the date;
unsure means `Planned`. Owner rule 2026-11-23 applies to every row: one fact, one place to enter it, one table, one
service that answers. Updated 2026-11-24 after the build: Built rows cite the real files and tests; Planned rows say what is missing. Pharmacy (WDR-050 to WDR-054) was approved by the owner on 2026-11-24 and built the same day (additive only); WDR-055 (hospital-network prescriptions) shares the same prescription service and is still to be confirmed end to end. Deliberate build choices: self-treatment has its own table self_treatments; withdrawal_until_* columns stay unread; herd prescription = whole herd; any farm member who can record may confirm a warning; medicated-feed course runs by calendar. Line numbers are as of 2026-11-24; "unverified" means not read or not run.
